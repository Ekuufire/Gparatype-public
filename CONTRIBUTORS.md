## Scientific Foundation

Gparatype builds upon published research describing the capsule biosynthesis
loci and molecular serotyping of *Glaesserella parasuis* (formerly
*Haemophilus parasuis*), including the foundational studies by Howell and
colleagues.

These publications are cited in the **References** section of the main
[README](README.md).
